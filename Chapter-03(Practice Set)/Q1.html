Swiggy - Food Delivery Partner

About Swiggy

Swiggy is a leading food delivery platform that connects customers with a wide range of restaurants and food outlets. With a user-friendly app and website, Swiggy allows users to order their favorite meals from the comfort of their homes.

Features

  • Wide variety of cuisines and restaurants to choose from.
  • Real-time order tracking for customers.
  • Multiple payment options including cash on delivery, credit/debit cards, and digital wallets.
  • Exclusive discounts and offers for users.

How to Order

  1. Download the Swiggy app or visit the website.
  2. Create an account or log in.
  3. Enter your location to see available restaurants.
  4. Select your desired restaurant and browse the menu.
  5. Add items to your cart and proceed to checkout.
  6. Choose your payment method and place your order.
